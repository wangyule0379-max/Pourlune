Privacy

Your puzzle stays here.

Progress, move history, settings and your statistics choice are saved in local device/browser storage. This game has no account or cloud sync.

Optional statistics

This build does not enable analytics or advertising and sends no gameplay statistics to GameMunchies or another receiver. Your choice is saved on this device. Connecting services requires an updated notice and a new choice.

Keeping and removing data

Local records remain until you or your platform clear the app/site data. A damaged save is kept until you explicitly choose to replace it; a retained recovery copy is not a cloud backup.

Control at any time

Change your choice in Settings. Declining never prevents play. No events are queued while statistics is unavailable or declined.
