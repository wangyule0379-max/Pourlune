# New App Review Preparation


Team standard added 2026-11-07: prepare this pack for every new app **before its first formal App Store review**. Apple may request these materials under Guideline 2.1; this is our proactive preparation standard, not a claim that every submission universally requires a video. It does not add a new TestFlight upload procedure or authorize submission. For updates, refresh affected evidence rather than repeating unchanged work. Mark non-applicable flows explicitly with a reason; do not add features to satisfy the template.

- [ ] **1. Physical-device recording and QA.** Record the intended review build on a supported physical device running the latest publicly released supported OS. Start with launching the app and show its typical end-to-end flow. Include account creation/login/deletion, UGC reporting/blocking, and paid access only where present. Record device, OS, build and date; keep supported-platform QA results separately. A 3–5 minute demonstration is a useful target, not an Apple duration requirement. Simulator, browser, mock SDK and compilation evidence cannot be labeled physical-device verification. Provide an accessible video link in Review Notes and/or a supported attachment; check that a reviewer can open it without requesting permission. This is review evidence, not an App Preview marketing video.
- [ ] **2. Purpose, audience and value.** Explain what the app does, whom it serves, the need it addresses and its business model in concrete terms. Avoid unsupported medical, financial or other claims.
- [ ] **3. Feature access instructions.** Provide reproducible navigation to core features and any setup, sample files or restricted areas. Supply a dedicated working review account only when needed, through App Store Connect's review fields; never put production credentials in the repository. Explicitly state when no login, account, IAP or UGC exists. Confirm review contact details are populated without copying private contact data into public evidence.
- [ ] **4. External services.** List actual runtime services and their purposes: authentication, data, payments, advertising, analytics, AI, etc. Distinguish bundled/offline functionality and asset-authoring tools from runtime providers. Match SDK/configuration and privacy disclosures to the exact build; do not copy an obsolete app's service list.
- [ ] **5. Regional behavior.** Read the intended storefronts from the current submission. Explain any country-dependent content, access, ads, consent or payment behavior; otherwise explicitly confirm the applicable consistency. Do not infer regions from language or timezone, or claim identical ad availability worldwide.
- [ ] **6. Regulatory credentials and third-party rights.** State whether regulated services apply. Inventory protected music, sound, art, fonts, icons, engine and other material; retain provenance, license/authorization, attribution and modification notices where applicable. Attach relevant supporting documents. Do not treat an attribution file alone as proof of every underlying right, or invent a license purchase.

Pack closure: link these six entries to the exact source/build, notes, recording, evidence and owner. Verify store screenshots/description match that build. Distinguish prepared documents, verified behavior and pending physical-device evidence. Collect any genuinely necessary human login/device/rights actions in one request after completing independent preparation. Do not resubmit a known crash or describe untested behavior as passed.
