**App name:** Pourlune

**Apple subtitle:** Color sorting, cozy scenes

**Play short description:** Match colors. Uncover cozy scenes.

**Beta description:** Sort each color into a full bottle with its matching fixed tag and leave every buffer empty. Play 60 main puzzles and rotating daily puzzles, uncovering three cozy scenes along the way. Hint, Undo and Restart are free, and core play works offline. Main and daily puzzles save separately on this device. There is no account or cloud sync. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove your progress. Advertising is disabled in this beta candidate. Analytics and crash sharing are separate choices that start off; Remote Config may still process installation data.

**What to Test:** Start a puzzle and try legal and illegal pours. Use Hint, Undo and Restart, then leave and resume the puzzle. Try the daily puzzle and return to your main save. Complete levels, continue with Next, and inspect unlocked scene pieces in Collection. Check readable fixed tags, large text, reduced motion and settings. Relaunch the app after a normal save and check progress. Report crashes, lost progress, incorrect rewards, controls you cannot activate, unreadable content and sound interruptions. Do not intentionally delete data to test recovery; the app has no cloud backup.
