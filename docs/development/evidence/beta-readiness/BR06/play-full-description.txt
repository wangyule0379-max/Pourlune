A little color. A little calm.

Pourlune brings color-sorting puzzles to a bright knitted world. Tap a source bottle and a legal destination, plan your pours, and work toward a clear goal: every target bottle full of the color on its fixed tag, with every buffer bottle empty. A target tag stays fixed, but another color can enter temporarily while you solve the puzzle.

Explore 60 main puzzles and collect pieces for three cozy scenes. Daily play selects a puzzle from a built-in pool of 30 candidates using your device's local date. Main and daily progress have separate local saves.

Hint, Undo and Restart are free. Core puzzles work offline, and you can play at your own pace. Adjust sound, music, haptics and reduced motion in Settings. Symbols accompany color tags.

This beta is free and advertising is disabled. Extra Bottle ad rewards are unavailable. Analytics and crash sharing are separate optional settings, initially off; configured online services may still process installation and technical data.

Progress is stored on your device. There is no player account or cloud sync. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data or switching devices may lose progress. Reset Progress removes gameplay records while keeping preferences.
