No sign-in, test account, password, purchase or subscription is required. Launch Pourlune and tap Start to begin the tutorial. Tap a source bottle and a legal destination; follow the tutorial prompts. Targets must be full and match their fixed tags, with all buffers empty.

Core play, Hint, Undo and Restart work offline. Main and Daily have separate local saves; Collection displays scene pieces earned from main progress. Settings contains privacy, sound, music, haptics and reduced-motion choices. There is no account creation or cloud sync.

This beta disables advertising, including rewarded Extra Bottle access. Free helpers remain available. Analytics and crash sharing are separate opt-in choices, initially off. Firebase Remote Config may use installation infrastructure independently of those choices. No tracking option or ATT prompt is enabled in this candidate.
