Pourlune is a color-sorting puzzle game with a bright knitted world. Play 60 main puzzles, revisit daily puzzles selected from a built-in pool of 30, and collect pieces for three cozy scenes.

Tap a source bottle, then a legal destination. Fill every target bottle with the color on its fixed tag and leave every buffer bottle empty. Tags do not restrict temporary pours. Hint, Undo and Restart are free.

Core play works offline. Main and daily puzzles have separate local saves. Daily puzzles follow your device's local date. There is no player account or cloud sync; uninstalling or clearing app data may remove progress.

This beta is free and advertising is disabled. Analytics and crash sharing are separate settings, both initially off. Configured online services may still process installation and technical data. Extra Bottle ad rewards are unavailable in this beta.
