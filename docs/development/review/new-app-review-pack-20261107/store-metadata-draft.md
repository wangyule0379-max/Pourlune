## English description draft

Pourlune is a color-sorting puzzle game with a quiet garden atmosphere. Plan your pours, sort the colors and work through 100 levels at your own pace.

• Play the core puzzles offline, with progress saved on your device.
• Use free hints, undo and restart when you want to try a different approach.
• Explore changing bottle arrangements and level goals.
• Choose your music, sound, haptics and reduced-motion preferences in Settings.
• An optional rewarded ad can add one empty buffer bottle to the current round. Advertising requires an internet connection and availability. Interstitial ads may appear between eligible levels.

No account is required. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Optional gameplay statistics can be turned off in the privacy controls.
