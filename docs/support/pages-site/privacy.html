Pourlune privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026. Pourlune is operated by changjiang li. Contact wangyule0379@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. This policy describes Pourlune's current free beta on iOS and Android, with advertising disabled.

Data on your device

Pourlune stores puzzle progress, undo history, collection pieces and settings locally. Core puzzles and free Hint, Undo and Restart work offline. No player account, cloud sync, business server, payment or subscription flow is implemented. Reset Progress removes gameplay records and keeps preferences. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove progress. Local reset does not delete records already received by a service provider.

Configured online services

This candidate includes production Firebase client configuration. Firebase Remote Config fetches bounded feature flags and disabled puzzle IDs using Firebase installation infrastructure. This can process an installation identifier, IP/network and app/device information, country, language, time zone and version information independently of analytics sharing. Unavailable or invalid values preserve local defaults or the last validated values. Remote values do not change an active puzzle or revoke an earned reward.

Optional gameplay analytics

Analytics is initially off. If enabled, the app sends bounded gameplay events including puzzle ID/version, random local instance/request IDs, completion and effective foreground duration, helper counts, and save/reward/ad outcome categories. The app does not put full boards, email, feedback bodies or arbitrary exception messages in those manual events. SDK technical and automatic events can also exist. Advertising storage, advertising user-data consent, personalization consent and automatic screen reporting are disabled in this implementation. The iOS Analytics dependency is the no-AdID variant; this does not prove that every embedded SDK is silent.

Optional crash sharing

Crash sharing is a separate choice, initially off. Native crash information may be cached locally while sharing is off. Enabling sharing requests upload of unsent reports, which can include reports cached before the choice was enabled. Diagnostics include technical stack traces and app/device information. Turning sharing off stops new app upload requests and requests deletion of unsent reports. It cannot recall reports already queued or sent, and SDK calls do not provide delivery or deletion receipts.

Change your choices

Use Settings to change analytics and crash sharing independently. A failed local save or native synchronization is shown with Retry; app event sharing remains blocked while unresolved. Missing or invalid Firebase configuration disables those services. Turning analytics off does not disable Remote Config or all Firebase processing.

Advertising

Ad requests and Extra Bottle ad rewards are disabled in this beta. Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform are still embedded, and the Android package includes advertising-related permissions. SDK presence does not establish actual collection, and disabled ad requests do not establish zero native data processing. This candidate has no ATT prompt and enables no tracking option.

If advertising is enabled in a later version, the implemented flow uses the official Google eligibility/privacy-options interface and requests non-personalized ads. That configuration requires a new disclosure and runtime review before release; it is not available in this beta. Closing an ad alone does not grant a reward.

Support and testing feedback

Support links open an external browser or mail app only when selected. Pourlune does not automatically send email or upload a board. When a player voluntarily sends a report, the chosen mail or testing platform processes its contents and sender details under that provider's terms. Please omit passwords and unrelated personal information.

Operator and contact

changjiang li operates Pourlune and handles support and privacy requests at wangyule0379@gmail.com. There is no player account to delete. If you contact us, we receive your sender address and the information you choose to include.

Requests and retention

Local gameplay data stays on your device until you reset progress or remove app data. Preferences remain after Reset Progress. For data sent to online services, retention and deletion depend on the applicable service and the type of record; removing local progress does not recall that data. See the Firebase service-specific information linked below. Support correspondence is used to handle the request and retained as needed for follow-up, security or applicable legal obligations.

Email the contact above to ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information you have provided. We may ask for the minimum information needed to identify the relevant record; do not send passwords or verification codes. Some SDK data may not be identifiable from a support email alone. We will explain limitations rather than promise that local reset or a support request can erase all provider records or security logs.

Service providers and international processing

Google provides Firebase services for remote configuration and, when you choose them, analytics and crash sharing. GitHub provides this website. Apple or Google process feedback sent through their testing platforms; your email provider processes messages you send. Provider infrastructure may process information outside your country. Their privacy and service-specific retention information is linked below.

Children and privacy questions

Pourlune does not request a player's name, email or birth date during gameplay. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support or online services, contact us so we can assess the request. Store age ratings and testing availability are provided by the applicable store.

Website and providers

Service disclosures: Firebase privacy, Firebase Apple data disclosure, Firebase Android data disclosure, Google Mobile Ads privacy. Vendor documentation is input to this policy, not a guarantee of network delivery.
