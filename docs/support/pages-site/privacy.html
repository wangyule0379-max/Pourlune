Pourlune privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026. Pourlune is operated by changjiang li. Contact wangyule0379@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. This policy covers Pourlune on iOS and Android. Online services depend on the version and configuration you use: iOS TestFlight 0.5.0 builds 16 and 17 use Google test advertisements and consent checks; version 0.5.0(18), when made available with GameMunchies configured, also supports the GameMunchies processing described below; Android closed-test 0.5.0(13) has advertising disabled. Test advertisements still involve network requests and data processing. Neither status means commercial advertising has been enabled.

Data on your device

Pourlune stores puzzle progress, undo history, collection pieces and settings locally. Core puzzles and free Hint, Undo and Restart work offline. There is no player account, cloud sync, payment or subscription. Optional online reporting is separate from your local game save. Reset Progress removes gameplay records and keeps preferences. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove progress. Local reset does not delete records already received by a service provider.

Configured online services

Distributed builds with Firebase configured use the following services. Firebase Remote Config fetches bounded feature flags and disabled puzzle IDs using Firebase installation infrastructure. This can process an installation identifier, IP/network and app/device information, country, language, time zone and version information independently of analytics sharing. Unavailable or invalid values preserve local defaults or the last validated values. Remote values do not change an active puzzle or revoke an earned reward.

Optional gameplay analytics

Analytics is initially off. If enabled, the app sends bounded gameplay events including puzzle ID/version, random local instance/request IDs, completion and effective foreground duration, helper counts, and save/reward/ad outcome categories. The app does not put full boards, email, feedback bodies or arbitrary exception messages in those manual events. SDK technical and automatic events can also exist. Advertising storage, advertising user-data consent, personalization consent and automatic screen reporting are disabled in this implementation. The iOS Analytics dependency is the no-AdID variant; this does not prove that every embedded SDK is silent.

GameMunchies statistics and ad settings

GameMunchies is not enabled in iOS builds 16 and 17 or Android closed-test build 13. Starting with version 0.5.0(18), when configured, the app connects to our GameMunchies service at api.gamemunchies.com. Optional gameplay statistics remain off initially and require your saved Analytics choice. Separate integration-test builds use a separate test game and environment; their records are not copied into production statistics.

The SDK records activity, newly started puzzle instances, actual ad impressions and ad-load failures. Resuming a saved puzzle, returning to the foreground or adding a bottle does not create a new-round event. Each event contains a random installation identifier, event identifier, event type, environment label and first occurrence time. The request also identifies the registered game and platform. These events do not include your email, name, advertising ID, puzzle number, board contents or reward details. Network infrastructure handles connection information such as your IP address; the service uses IP information for rate limiting but does not put it in the gameplay-event record.

GameMunchies also fetches bounded ad settings independently of Analytics: whether advertisements and the existing next-level and extra-bottle scenes are enabled, and the interstitial interval. These requests use the registered game, platform and environment and involve network/security processing even while optional statistics are off. The app does not create a GameMunchies installation identifier solely to fetch settings. Cached or default settings allow offline puzzles to continue. Settings cannot revoke an already earned reward or bypass local consent and safety limits.

The SDK stores an installation identifier and a bounded pending queue on the device, with a maximum of 1,000 events or 512 KiB. Events older than 30 days are discarded during subsequent queue processing. Server cleanup targets records older than 30 days and runs in batches, so deletion is not guaranteed at the exact age boundary. Infrastructure security logs have separate handling. In version 0.5.0(18), turning Analytics off closes new collection and sending and clears the current service scope's pending queue. The app attempts to cancel in-flight statistics requests; it cannot recall records already received by the server. The stored installation identifier can remain, and Reset Progress does not clear this reporting storage. Previously collected events cleared on withdrawal are not resent when you turn Analytics on again. Contact us about access or deletion; do not send passwords or account tokens.

Optional crash sharing

Crash sharing is a separate choice, initially off. Native crash information may be cached locally while sharing is off. Enabling sharing requests upload of unsent reports, which can include reports cached before the choice was enabled. Diagnostics include technical stack traces and app/device information. Turning sharing off stops new app upload requests and requests deletion of unsent reports. It cannot recall reports already queued or sent, and SDK calls do not provide delivery or deletion receipts.

Change your choices

Use Settings to change analytics and crash sharing independently. A failed local save is shown with Retry and blocks new app event sharing until the choice is saved. A provider synchronization failure is also shown with Retry; other enabled reporting services may continue under your saved choices. Missing or invalid Firebase configuration disables Firebase services, independently of GameMunchies. Turning Analytics off does not disable remote settings or all network processing.

Advertising and consent

When an ad-enabled version is used, Google Mobile Ads requests and displays advertisements, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks consent eligibility and presents applicable privacy choices. In the current iOS test version these are official Google test advertisements. In the current Android closed-test version, ad requests and UMP checks are disabled; Extra bottle can grant one buffer per puzzle instance in a local save transaction without pretending an advertisement was watched.

Google's advertising and consent services can process IP-derived approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, consent management and fraud prevention. The exact identifiers depend on the platform, permissions and consent. The current Android manifest removes the advertising-ID permission; iOS uses no-AdID Firebase Analytics and does not request ATT tracking authorization. These restrictions do not remove all SDK identifiers or other data processing. A non-personalized or test ad is not a promise of zero data collection.

The app requests non-personalized advertisements and blocks requests until UMP reports that ads may be requested. Where UMP requires privacy options, they remain available through the app's Settings. Analytics and Crash sharing are separate choices; switching them off does not itself switch off consent checks or eligible ad requests. Ad eligibility is also not treated as permission to enable optional gameplay analytics.

Unavailable consent, inventory or network does not block core puzzles or free Hint, Undo and Restart. An ad-based Extra Bottle reward requires the SDK's actual earned-reward callback and a successful local save; closing an advertisement alone is not a reward. SDK and store privacy links below explain Google's processing and choices.

Support and testing feedback

Support links open an external browser or mail app only when selected. Pourlune does not automatically send email or upload a board. When a player voluntarily sends a report, the chosen mail or testing platform processes its contents and sender details under that provider's terms. Please omit passwords and unrelated personal information.

Operator and contact

Requests and retention

Local gameplay data stays on your device until you reset progress or remove app data. Preferences remain after Reset Progress. For data sent to online services, retention and deletion depend on the applicable service and the type of record; removing local progress does not recall that data. See the Firebase service-specific information linked below. Support correspondence is used to handle the request and retained as needed for follow-up, security or applicable legal obligations.

Email the contact above to ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information you have provided. We may ask for the minimum information needed to identify the relevant record; do not send passwords or verification codes. Some SDK data may not be identifiable from a support email alone. We will explain limitations rather than promise that local reset or a support request can erase all provider records or security logs.

Children and privacy questions

Pourlune is intended for players aged 13 and older and does not request a player's name, email or birth date during gameplay. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support or online services, contact us so we can assess the request. Store age ratings and testing availability are provided by the applicable store.
