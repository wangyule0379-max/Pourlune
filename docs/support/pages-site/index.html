How to play · Saves · Daily puzzles · Report a problem

How to play

Tap a source bottle, then a legal destination. Fill every target bottle with the color on its fixed tag and leave all buffer bottles empty. Tags stay fixed but allow another color to enter temporarily while you solve the puzzle.

Hint, Undo and Restart are free. Hint suggests a move without pouring for you. A hint can time out; that does not prove the puzzle is impossible. If no suggestion appears, you can still Undo, Restart or continue yourself.

Save and resume

Core play works offline. Each accepted action saves locally. If saving fails, keep the app open and use Retry. Wait for a successful save before continuing. Main and daily puzzles have separate local saves.

There is no player account or cloud sync. Uninstalling the app, clearing app data or switching devices may lose progress. Reset Progress removes gameplay records and keeps preferences. It does not recall data already received by online services.

Daily puzzles and collections

Daily play follows your device's local date and selects from 30 built-in candidates; it does not download 30 new puzzles each day. When a new date is available, use the in-app choice to keep or replace the saved daily puzzle. Daily Replay does not grant the same daily reward again. Scene pieces come from main progress across 60 main puzzles and three scenes.

Settings and this beta

Sound, music, haptics and reduced motion can be adjusted in Settings. Color tags have symbols as well as color. Analytics and crash sharing are separate choices and initially off. Remote Config can still use installation infrastructure; see the privacy policy.

Build 0.5.0+7 has advertising disabled. Beta builds explicitly marked for ad testing use Google's official test ads for the existing Extra Bottle and level-transition flows. Availability depends on privacy eligibility, network and inventory; free helpers remain available. Closing an ad alone does not earn a reward.

Report a problem

After you have received a test invitation, use TestFlight Send Beta Feedback or the Google Play private testing feedback option. You can also email the support contact below. The app never sends a board or feedback automatically.

  • App version and build, device model, and OS version.
  • Main level ID or the daily date shown in the app.
  • Steps to reproduce, what you expected, and what happened. Include the exact save error if one is shown.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, private account documents or unrelated photos. If you choose to attach a screenshot through the testing platform, check it for personal information first.
