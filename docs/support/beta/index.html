Pourlune beta help

Pourlune is an offline color-sorting puzzle game. Match every full target bottle to its fixed tag and leave all buffers empty. Tags stay fixed but do not prevent another color from temporarily entering the bottle.

Tap a source bottle and a legal destination. Hint, Undo and Restart are free. A hint may time out without proving that a puzzle is impossible. Main and daily puzzles have separate local saves. Daily puzzles follow the device's local date.

Each accepted action saves locally. If saving fails, keep the app open and use Retry. Wait for a successful save before continuing. Reset Progress removes gameplay records and retains preferences. There is no player account or cloud sync; uninstalling, clearing data or changing devices may lose progress.

This beta candidate disables advertising. Extra Bottle rewards cannot be earned from an advertisement in this configuration. Free helpers remain available. Analytics and crash sharing are separate settings and start off. Firebase Remote Config may still use installation infrastructure.

To report a beta problem, use TestFlight Send Beta Feedback or Google Play private testing feedback after distribution is actually enabled. Include the app build, device/OS, level or daily date, what happened and the steps to reproduce it. Do not include passwords, private photos or account documents. A direct support mailbox and response owner have not yet been approved.

Accessibility settings include reduced motion, sound/music and haptics preferences, and color tags use symbols as well as color. Actual screen-reader behavior, physical sound/haptics and representative device performance remain under validation.
